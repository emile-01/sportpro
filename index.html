How it's calculated

Expected goals for the home team = home scored × away conceded ÷ league home average. For the away team it is away scored × home conceded ÷ league away average.

Each side's goal count is then modelled with the Poisson formula P(k) = e−λλk / k!. Multiplying the two gives the chance of every exact score.
