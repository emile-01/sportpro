Instant

Profit & loss, live

Experience the power of real-time financial tracking without the hassle of complex spreadsheets. Our intuitive interface allows you to simply enter your revenue, cost of goods sold (COGS), and operating expenses to instantly generate a comprehensive view of your business's health. As you type, watch your gross and net profit figures update immediately, giving you instant clarity on your bottom line. We automatically calculate your profit margins, eliminating manual math errors and saving you valuable time. Whether you are testing a new pricing strategy or reviewing your monthly performance, this live tool provides the immediate feedback you need to make confident, data-driven decisions on the fly.

Built in

Break-even calculator

Understanding exactly when your business turns a profit is critical for long-term success, which is why we have integrated a powerful break-even calculator directly into our platform. By simply setting your price per unit and your variable costs, you can instantly determine the exact number of sales required to cover your total expenses. This feature removes the guesswork from financial planning, allowing you to visualize the precise moment your venture shifts from a loss to a profit. It is an essential tool for setting realistic sales targets, validating business models, and understanding the financial viability of your products before you even launch them.

Saved

Your recent calculations

Never lose track of a critical financial scenario again with our automatic saving feature. Every calculation you run is securely stored locally on your device, ensuring your sensitive financial data remains private while being instantly accessible. This means you can easily reload previous scenarios, compare past performance against current numbers, and track how your financial outlook changes over time. With just one tap, you can revisit an old pricing model or expense projection without having to re-enter all the data. It creates a convenient, historical log of your financial planning sessions, making it simple to analyze trends and refine your strategy.
